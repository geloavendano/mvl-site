How to set up the livestream

Courtside checklist for camera → OBS → YouTube Live.

Choose camera
1Camera, gear, and placement
  • Mount the DJI Pocket 3 on a tall tripod, centered behind one baseline and elevated 2–3 metres.
  • Connect it to the laptop using a USB-C data cable, choose Webcam on the camera, and keep it powered throughout the match.
  • Frame the full court in a static wide shot. Keep ActiveTrack off and avoid digital zoom.
  • Mount the Sony a6000 on a tall tripod, centered behind one baseline and elevated 2–3 metres.
  • Use movie mode, a wide lens, manual focus and exposure, and set HDMI Info Display: Off for a clean feed.
  • Connect micro-HDMI → capture card → laptop USB 3.0. Use a dummy battery and add a separate USB/DJI Mic 2 audio source in OBS.
2Add the camera and scoreboard in OBS
  1. In OBS, choose + → Video Capture Device.
  2. Select the DJI Pocket 3 and fit it to the 1920 × 1080 canvas.
  3. Add the correct MVL scoreboard Browser Source and confirm that it sits bottom-center with a safe gap.

Base and output1920 × 1080

4Connect YouTube and go live
  1. In OBS, open Settings → Stream, choose YouTube – RTMPS, and connect the channel account.
  2. In YouTube Studio, set the category to Sports and latency to Normal. Use Unlisted for rehearsal and Public for the match.
  3. Start streaming from OBS and wait for YouTube stream health to show Good or Excellent.
  4. Copy the live YouTube URL into the correct Court field above, turn on Live now, then select Save livestreams.
5Before first serve
  • Upload speed is around 8 Mbps or better from the actual streaming position.
  • Camera power, framing, focus, exposure, and audio are stable.
  • An Unlisted end-to-end test has been completed.
  • Scoreboard overlay and the correct court controls are loaded.
  • YouTube stream health is Good or Excellent.
  • Backup hotspot or second SIM is ready.
