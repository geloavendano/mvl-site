- [ ] Collect each player's full display name, surname, jersey number, role, and photo.

- [ ] Confirm the complete preliminary and playoff schedule.
- [ ] Freeze the registration form, waiver language, tournament rules, and FAQs.
- [ ] Test registration and waiver submissions from mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Confirm the registration close date and the process for approved late changes.
- [ ] Confirm who receives registration exports and who owns roster corrections.
- [ ] Verify the production domain, SSL, analytics, social links, and contact links.

## August 1–21 — Content completion and tournament-page preparation

- [ ] Reconcile submitted registrations against the official team rosters.
- [ ] Resolve duplicate jersey numbers and incomplete player records.
- [ ] Complete player-photo naming and upload checks.
- [ ] Confirm sponsors, sponsorship tiers, logo files, and website links.
- [ ] Confirm tournament venue details, court names, dates, and start times.
- [ ] Confirm the final scoring, ranking, and playoff-seeding rules.
- [ ] Review all website copy with tournament organizers.
- [ ] Prepare the YouTube channel, livestream events, thumbnails, and playlists.

- [ ] Publish the confirmed sponsors and final team list.
- [ ] Complete the public schedule, standings, game-video, and rules pages.
- [ ] Confirm all Player of the Game roster lookups and portraits.
- [ ] Add and test every approved Google SSO admin email.

- [ ] Test livestream on/off behavior on the homepage.
- [ ] Test YouTube links and inline playback on the Videos page.

## Before August 29 — Day 1 final checks

- [ ] Apply any approved schedule adjustments.

- [ ] Confirm every player's surname, jersey number, and photo.
- [ ] Confirm the official Day 1 livestream URL.

- [ ] Keep a backup internet connection available.
- [ ] Export or record a clean pre-tournament database snapshot.

## Before each tournament day — August 29, 30, 31, September 5, and 6

- [ ] Confirm the day's game order, courts, and start times.
- [ ] Apply approved roster or schedule changes before the first game.
- [ ] Confirm the livestream event and YouTube URL.
- [ ] Confirm the scorekeeper, result approver, and media uploader on duty.
- [ ] Verify admin sign-in, venue internet, charger, and backup device.
- [ ] Check the homepage, schedule, standings, and Videos page.

## During each tournament day

- [ ] Turn on the livestream setting and add the correct livestream link.
- [ ] After each game, enter all set scores and the winner.
- [ ] Enter the Player of the Game using `surname-jersey`, such as `santos-04`.
- [ ] Add or update the YouTube recording link for the game.
- [ ] Have a second operator verify the score and player before publishing.
- [ ] Confirm the public schedule and standings updated correctly.
- [ ] Confirm the recording appears and plays on the Videos page.
- [ ] Correct reported errors promptly and note what was changed.

## Special playoff checks — September 5 and 6

- [ ] After the last preliminary game, verify all eight final seeds.
- [ ] Verify the four Quarter-Final matchups before play starts.
- [ ] Verify the one-set advantage if a team finishes 7–0.
- [ ] After the Quarter-Finals, verify both Semi-Final matchups.
- [ ] After the Semi-Finals, verify the Bronze and Championship matchups.
- [ ] Do not start score entry until the displayed teams match the official bracket.

## End of each tournament day

- [ ] Turn off the livestream setting.
- [ ] Audit every completed game for winner, set scores, Player of the Game, and video.
- [ ] List missing or delayed recordings for follow-up.
- [ ] Verify standings and next-day matchups with tournament operations.
- [ ] Save a daily results export or database snapshot.
- [ ] Post the final public link for the day's schedule and videos.

## After the tournament

- [ ] Verify the final standings, medalists, and champion.
- [ ] Create and publish the tournament photo gallery page.
- [ ] Upload and organize official photos by day or album.
- [ ] Replace the homepage header image with the champion team image.
- [ ] Feature the Championship, Bronze, and selected highlight videos.
- [ ] Archive or remove obsolete livestream links.
- [ ] Export the final games, scores, standings, player awards, and video links.
- [ ] Back up all player photos and event media references.
- [ ] Remove temporary admin access and retain only long-term maintainers.
- [ ] Test all permanent pages and repair broken media links.

## Recommended live-maintenance ownership

- **Data operator:** enters set scores, winners, and Player of the Game.
- **Result approver:** checks the official scoresheet before/after publication.
- **Media operator:** manages livestream and game-recording links.
- **Target update time:** publish verified results within 5–10 minutes of each game.
- **Correction rule:** never silently overwrite a disputed result; confirm it with the official scoresheet first.
